How to play

Tap a bus in the parking lot. If the way ahead is clear, it drives out in the direction of its arrow and pulls into a free bay. The passenger at the front of the queue boards a bus of the same color, and full buses leave to free the bay. If all 5 bays fill up and no bus fits the next passenger, you lose. Carry everyone to finish the level.

450 levels across 50 districts

Every district is harder than the last: bigger lots, more colors, long buses, buses that block each other and mystery buses whose color is revealed only when their path clears. The last level of each district is a hard level. Every level is tested to be solvable.
