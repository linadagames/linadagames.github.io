Privacy Policy

Bus Depot Puzzle

Effective date: November 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Linada Games ("we", "us") handles information in the mobile game Bus Depot Puzzle (package name com.linadagames.busdepotpuzzle) and its web version (the "Game").

1. Information we collect

We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, e-mail address, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location.

The Game saves the following only on your device so you can continue where you left off: level progress, stars, coins, daily reward streak, language and sound settings. This data is not sent to us or to any server. It is deleted when you uninstall the Game or clear its data.

2. Advertising

The Game is free and is supported by ads. Ads are provided by Google AdMob (Google LLC). To show, measure and improve ads and to prevent fraud, Google may automatically collect and process information such as:

  • your device's advertising ID (Android Advertising ID) and other device identifiers,
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it,
  • device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version),
  • ad interactions (for example, which ads were shown or tapped) and diagnostic data.

Google acts as an independent controller of this data. Learn more in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Where required by law (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland), we ask for your consent before personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your Android settings (Settings › Google › Ads or Settings › Privacy › Ads, depending on your device).

Optional "watch an ad" rewards (such as extra coins) are always your choice.

3. Internet access

The Game can be played offline. Internet access is used only to load ads.

4. How information is shared

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with third parties for their own purposes, other than the advertising partner described above.

5. Children

The Game is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the Game, please contact us and we will help remove it.

6. Data retention and security

Game progress stays on your device until you delete it. Data collected by Google for advertising is retained according to Google's policies. We use the security measures provided by the Android platform and Google Play.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or Turkey's KVKK), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your personal data. Because we do not store personal data on our servers, most requests can be handled by uninstalling the Game or using your device's ad settings. For advertising data, you can also contact Google. For any questions, contact us at the address below.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The new version will be published on this page with an updated effective date.
