How to play

Swap two neighboring gems to line up three or more of the same kind. Match four for a striped gem that clears a row or column, an L or T shape for a glowing gem that blasts its surroundings, and five for a rainbow orb that clears a whole color. Swap two special gems for a super combo. Melt ice, crack rocks and break chains to reach each level's goal before you run out of moves.

A garden full of jewels

Travel through 25 zones, from the Ruby Valley to the Amber Desert and beyond, and earn up to 3 stars on every level. Use the hammer, shuffle, magic wand and extra moves when a level gets tough, and come back every day for bigger daily rewards.
