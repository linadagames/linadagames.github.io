Privacy Policy

Jewel Blocks (Android app com.linadagames.jewelblocks) · Last updated: November 21, 2026

Summary

The game does not require an account and does not ask for personal information. Your progress is stored on your device. The Android app shows ads from Google AdMob in endless mode, and AdMob may collect some data from your device to show and measure ads.

Information stored on your device

Your level progress, stars, best score and settings (language, sound effects, music, vibration) are stored only on your own device and are not sent to us. They are deleted when you clear the app's data or uninstall it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

In endless mode the app may offer an optional rewarded ad to continue a game, and shows a full-screen ad after some finished games. Ads are provided by AdMob, a service of Google LLC. To show and measure ads and prevent fraud, AdMob may collect and process:

  • device identifiers such as the Advertising ID,
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it,
  • device and app information, ad interactions (impressions, taps) and diagnostic data.

Users in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland are asked for consent through Google's consent form before personalized ads are shown, and can change their choice later. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.

Learn more: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Permissions

The app uses the internet and network state permissions (for ads) and vibration. It does not access your location, camera, microphone, contacts or files. The game itself works offline.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its date.
