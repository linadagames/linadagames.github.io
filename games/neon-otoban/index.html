Neon Otoban: Highway Racer

Floor it on an endless highway and see how far you can go before your car is scrap. Weave through traffic, fire your nitro and drift through the city, the desert and the coast.

Real driving feel

A 6-speed engine sound with gear shifts and exhaust backfires, a body that leans under braking and acceleration, and drifts: steer while braking to kick up tire smoke and fill your nitro.

Three routes, three times of day

Race through the skyscrapers of the Metropolis, the dunes and mesas of the Red Desert and the beaches and palm trees of the Blue Coast, by day, at sunset or at night.

Risk = reward

Skim past cars for near-miss bonuses and build combos. The oncoming lanes score 1.5x, but a head-on crash costs a life. Collect nitro, repair wrenches, coins, shields and extra lives along the way.

Garage

Seven cars, from a hot hatch and a tough SUV to an electric hypercar. Pick from 14 paint colors, racing stripes, rim colors and neon underglow, and earn credits every race to unlock new cars. Steer with touch buttons or by tilting your phone.
