Privacy Policy

Neon Otoban: Highway Racer (Android app com.linadagames.neonotoban) · Last updated: November 21, 2026

Summary

Neon Otoban (the “Game”) is free and shows ads through Google AdMob. The Game itself does not ask for an account, e-mail address or location.

Information stored on your device

Your high score, earned credits, selected car and settings are stored only on your device and are not sent to us. They are deleted when you uninstall the Game.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

To show ads, Google may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information and ad interactions. This data is subject to the Google Privacy Policy; see also How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. Users in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom are asked for consent for ads. You can turn off personalized ads or reset your advertising ID in your device's Settings › Google › Ads.

Rewarded ads (to continue a run or double your credits) are optional and are shown only when you choose to watch them.

Permissions and sensors

The vibration permission is used for feedback when you crash. If you choose tilt steering, the device's motion sensor is read in real time only to steer and is not recorded. The Game does not access your location, camera, microphone, contacts or files.

Children

The Game does not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its date.
