How to play

Swipe left and right to change lanes, swipe up to jump and swipe down to slide. Dodge oncoming trains, jump over barriers and slide under gates.

Go further, score higher

Collect coins and grab magnet and shield power-ups. The further you run, the faster it gets and the higher your score multiplier climbs. A day and night cycle, rain and storms keep every run different.
