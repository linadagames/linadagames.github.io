Privacy Policy

Neon Ray Runner (Android app com.linadagames.neonrayrunner) · Last updated: November 21, 2026

Summary

The game does not require an account and does not ask for personal information. To stay free, it shows ads from Google AdMob. AdMob may collect some data from your device to show and measure ads.

Information stored on your device

Your high score, total coins and settings (sound, graphics, language) are stored only on your own device and are not sent to us. They are deleted when you uninstall the app.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Ads are provided by AdMob, a service of Google LLC. AdMob may collect and process:

  • device identifiers such as the Advertising ID,
  • approximate location derived from your IP address,
  • ad interactions (impressions, taps) and app crash and performance information.

This data is used to show ads, prevent fraud and measure performance. Users in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland are shown a consent form on first launch and can change their choice at any time from the Privacy settings link in the game menu. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.

Learn more: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Permissions

The app uses only the internet (for ads) and vibration permissions. It does not access your location, camera, microphone, contacts or files.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its date.
