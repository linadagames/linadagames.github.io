How to play

Drag a block anywhere it fits. When it touches a gate of its color that is wide enough, it slides off the board. Clear every block before the clock runs out.

New twists as you go

One-way blocks, frozen blocks, locks and keys, swinging gates and layered blocks keep every chapter fresh. Each chapter of 20 levels brings a new kind of wood.
