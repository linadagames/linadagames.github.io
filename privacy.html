Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Linada Games (“we”, “us”) handles information in all of our games and on this website. It applies to the following apps, including their Android versions published on Google Play and any future iOS versions on the App Store (together, the “Games”):

  • Timber Gates: Wood Jam (com.linadagames.timbergates)
  • Bus Depot Puzzle (com.linadagames.busdepotpuzzle)
  • Neon Ray Runner (com.linadagames.neonrayrunner)
  • Jewel Blocks (com.mucevherbloklari.game)

When we release a new game, it is covered by this policy and added to this list.

1. Summary

Our Games have no accounts and no sign-in. We do not collect your name, e-mail address, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location. Your game progress is stored on your own device. We do not run our own servers that receive data from the Games. The Games are free and show ads provided by Google AdMob, which collects some device data to show and measure ads, as described below.

2. Information stored on your device

To let you continue where you left off, each Game saves data such as level progress, stars, scores, coins, lives, daily reward streaks and settings (language, sound, music, vibration, graphics, reminders) only on your device. Some Games also keep simple usage counters (for example, how many levels were started or finished) on the device. This data is not sent to us. It is deleted when you clear the app's data or uninstall the Game.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Games are supported by ads from Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. Depending on the Game, these may be optional rewarded ads that you choose to watch (for example, for extra coins or to continue) and occasional full-screen ads between levels or games. To show, measure and improve ads and to prevent fraud, Google may automatically collect and process information such as:

  • your device's advertising ID and other device identifiers,
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it,
  • device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version),
  • ad interactions (for example, which ads were shown or tapped) and diagnostic data.

Google acts as an independent controller of this data. Learn more in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Where required by law (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland), the Games ask for your consent through Google's consent form before personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice later from the privacy options in the Game's settings or menu. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your device settings.

4. Notifications

Some Games (for example, Timber Gates) can schedule local reminders on your device, such as a daily reminder or a note when your lives are full, if you allow notifications. They are created on the device and no data is sent for them. You can turn them off in the Game's settings or in your device settings.

5. Device backup

On Android, your game data may be included in your phone's own Google backup (Android Auto Backup) if you have it turned on. That backup is stored in your Google account by Google under Google's terms; we cannot access it. You can turn it off in your phone's settings.

6. Permissions

The Games use only the permissions they need: internet and network state (to load ads), vibration, and, where a Game offers reminders, notifications. The Games do not access your location, camera, microphone, contacts or files. Most of the Games can be played offline; internet access is used to load ads.

7. How information is shared

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with third parties for their own purposes, other than the advertising partner described above.

8. Children

The Games are not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through a Game, please contact us and we will help remove it.

9. Data retention and security

Game data stays on your device until you delete it. Data collected by Google for advertising is retained according to Google's policies. We rely on the security measures provided by the device platform and the app stores.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or Turkey's KVKK), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your personal data. Because we do not store personal data on our own servers, most requests can be handled by clearing the app's data, uninstalling the Game or using your device's ad settings. For advertising data, you can also contact Google. For any questions, contact us at the address below.

11. This website

This website (linadagames.github.io) has no ads, no accounts and no tracking. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log basic technical information such as your IP address for security, and its pages load fonts from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address as part of that request.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, for example when we release a new game. The new version will be published on this page with an updated date.
